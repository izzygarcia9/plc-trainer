AE-3: Material Age Clock — Tank A-15
← Hub
Challenge: Design a ladder logic program to keep track of the age of the material in tank A-15 in days, hours, and minutes. There are only two controls for this clock: A15_CLK_STT starts the clock by resetting it and then allowing it to run and A15_CLK_STP stops the clock and holds the current time. The days, hours, and minutes are retained in A15_DAY, A15_HR, and A15_MIN, respectively.
